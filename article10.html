📌 Savings Tips for Small Business Owners in Tanzania

Date:

Introduction

Small business owners are the backbone of Tanzania's economy. However, many lack the habit of saving and face financial challenges. Saving is not just about keeping money aside; it's about protecting yourself against emergencies and building a foundation for growth.

💡 Key Insight: Savings are like a "tree" you plant today to harvest tomorrow. Every small start counts, but persistence pays off.

1. Set Aside a Percentage of Your Daily Income

Many business owners make the mistake of saving only "what's left" after expenses. Instead, save first before you spend.

📊 Example: If you earn TZS 50,000 per day, save 10% (TZS 5,000) before spending anything else.

2. Use a Separate Savings Account

Don't mix your savings with your business money. Open a separate bank account or mobile wallet for your savings.

  • NMB, CRDB, and NBC offer special savings accounts.
  • You can also use mobile wallets like M-Pesa or Tigo Pesa.

3. Avoid Unnecessary Expenses

Before buying something, ask yourself: "Do I really need this?". Unnecessary expenses can ruin your savings plan.

4. Use Kikotoo cha Makato to See Your Fees

When you send or withdraw money, fees eat into your money. Use the Kikotoo cha Makato app to see fees before you transact. This will help you save more.

5. Set Savings Goals

Setting goals helps you stay focused. For example:

  • Short-term goal: Save TZS 200,000 in 3 months.
  • Long-term goal: Save TZS 2,000,000 in 1 year.

6. Join a VICOBA or SACCO

VICOBA and SACCO groups are great ways to save. Members meet regularly and contribute with discipline.

7. Collect Your Payments On Time

Some customers owe you money. Make sure you collect your payments quickly. Uncollected money is lost money.

8. Learn from Others

Visit other successful business owners and learn how they save. Also, read articles and educate yourself.

9. Avoid High-Interest Loans

Many mobile loans have 4% monthly interest, which is high. Avoid taking loans for unnecessary expenses. Your savings are cheaper than a loan.

10. Practice Financial Discipline

Saving requires discipline. Others may try to convince you to spend, but stick to your goals.

Conclusion

Having savings is an investment in your life and business. No amount is too small — every cent counts. Start today, and reap the rewards tomorrow.

💡 Remember: "Don't save what is left after spending; spend what is left after saving." — Warren Buffett
