⚠️ Common Mistakes That Cost You Money on Mobile Transactions

Date:

Introduction

Many Tanzanians lose money on mobile transactions because of common mistakes. In this article, we'll highlight these mistakes and show you how to avoid them.

Mistake #1: Not Comparing Fees

Many people use the same provider without checking if another provider offers cheaper fees. This can cost you thousands of shillings every month.

Solution: Use the Kikotoo cha Makato app to compare fees before every transaction.

Mistake #2: Withdrawing Cash When You Don't Need To

Withdrawing cash is expensive. If you can pay directly using your mobile wallet, you'll save on withdrawal fees.

Solution: Pay directly from your wallet whenever possible.

Mistake #3: Sending Money Without Checking the Number

Sending money to the wrong number can be a costly mistake, especially if you're sending a large amount.

Solution: Always double-check the number before confirming the transaction.

Mistake #4: Ignoring Promotions

Many providers offer promotions that can save you money. Ignoring them is a missed opportunity.

Solution: Stay updated on promotions from your provider.

Mistake #5: Not Using the Kikotoo cha Makato App

The Kikotoo cha Makato app helps you avoid these mistakes by showing you fees, comparing providers, and checking numbers for fraud.

Solution: Use the app before every transaction.

💡 Key Takeaway: By avoiding these common mistakes, you can save up to TZS 50,000 per month!
