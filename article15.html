Introduction

In Tanzania, mobile money is a key part of everyday life. However, many people don't realize how much they're losing by not comparing fees.

Why Fees Matter

Fees may seem small individually, but they add up over time. For example, if you lose TZS 2,000 per day on fees, that's TZS 60,000 per month.

How to Compare Fees

Use the Kikotoo cha Makato app to compare fees across different providers. The app shows you exact fees and recommends the cheapest option.
