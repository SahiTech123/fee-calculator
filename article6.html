Introduction

Every day, millions of Tanzanians send and receive money through mobile money services like M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, and Airtel Money. While these services are convenient, many people are unaware of the fees they are paying. In fact, studies show that over 94% of Tanzanians do not know the exact fees charged on their transactions.

These fees may seem small individually, but they add up over time. For example, someone who makes 10 transactions per day can lose up to TZS 100,000 per month just on mobile money fees.

In this article, we will explore how you can save money by simply comparing mobile money fees before making transactions. We will also show you how to use the Kikotoo cha Makato app to make smarter financial decisions.

Why Do People Lose Money on Fees?

There are two main reasons why people lose money on mobile money fees:

  • Lack of awareness: Many people do not know the exact fees charged by different providers.
  • Not comparing providers: Most people stick to one provider (e.g., M-Pesa) without checking if another provider offers cheaper rates.

How to Save Money

1. Compare Before You Send

Before sending money, check which provider offers the lowest fees. For example:

  • For amounts below TZS 30,000, Airtel Money offers the lowest fees.
  • For TZS 100,000, Selcom Pesa charges TZS 1,900.
  • For any amount, HaloPesa charges TZS 0 for HaloPesa-to-HaloPesa transfers.

2. Split Large Amounts

Sometimes, splitting a large amount into smaller parts can help you save on fees. For example:

  • TZS 300,000 once: Fee TZS 4,500
  • Split into TZS 150,000 + TZS 150,000: Fee TZS 3,150
  • You save: TZS 1,350!

3. Take Advantage of Promotions

  • Airtel Money: Free transfers between Airtel users via the app.
  • Selcom Pesa: Special promotions of TZS 500 for 5 daily transactions.

4. Use a Fee Calculator Tool

Instead of calculating manually, use the Kikotoo cha Makato app to instantly compare fees across all providers before making any transaction. The app will show you:

  • Exact fees for each provider.
  • Recommendations for the cheapest option.
  • Smart split suggestions to save even more.

Conclusion

Saving money by comparing mobile money fees is not difficult. All you need is awareness and the right tools. By comparing providers and splitting transactions, you can save up to TZS 20,000 - 50,000 per month.
