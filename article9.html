📊 The Difference Between Sending and Withdrawing Money Fees

Date:

Introduction

Many Tanzanians don't realize that sending money and withdrawing money have different fees. Most people think the fees are the same, but the truth is that withdrawing money is more expensive than sending.

In this article, we'll show you the difference between sending and withdrawal fees for the major mobile money providers in Tanzania. This will help you save money by choosing the right transaction method.

💡 Tip: If possible, avoid withdrawing cash from agents. Instead, use your money for mobile transactions or bank payments to save on fees.

Key Difference Between Sending and Withdrawing

📤 Sending Money

Fee: Usually a small percentage of the amount you send.

Example: Sending TZS 100,000 with M-Pesa costs only TZS 350.

Advantage: Fees are low and transparent.

🏧 Withdrawing Money

Fee: Usually more expensive because you're withdrawing cash.

Example: Withdrawing TZS 100,000 with M-Pesa costs TZS 2,800.

Disadvantage: Fees are high and can be up to 3% of the amount.

Why is Withdrawing More Expensive?

  • Agent costs: Agents charge a fee for their service.
  • Government taxes: There are specific taxes on cash withdrawals.
  • Cash handling costs: Mobile network operators incur costs for transporting cash to agents.

How to Save Money

1. Avoid Withdrawing Unless Necessary

If you can, avoid withdrawing cash from agents. Instead, use your money for mobile transactions or direct payments.

2. Use Banks or Bank Agents

Withdrawing money through a bank or bank agent (like NMB, CRDB) can be cheaper than using a mobile money agent.

3. Compare Before You Withdraw

If you must withdraw, compare the fees of different providers. For example:

  • For TZS 100,000, AzamPesa charges only TZS 1,000.
  • For TZS 100,000, Selcom Pesa charges only TZS 1,900.

4. Split Large Amounts

As we saw in the first article, splitting large amounts can reduce your fees.
